Key achievements

JCI Accreditation leadership

Led the Medication Management Chapter during JCI Accreditation and contributed to a successful accreditation with zero major findings.

Operational standardization

Standardized pharmacy policies across three hospitals and five outpatient clinics into a simplified operational manual.

Medication safety reporting

Developed a hospital-wide reporting framework for medication errors, near misses, and ADRs.

Performance improvement

Reduced medication errors from approximately 10 per month to 3–4 and increased ADR reporting from 3 to 8–9 per month.

Medication safety communications

Developed and published a weekly Medication Safety Bulletin to strengthen awareness and learning.

High-alert medication control

Standardized labeling for high-alert, LASA, refrigerated, cytotoxic, and light-sensitive medications.

Inventory accountability

Established a pharmacist-led monthly inventory audit program to improve storage compliance and expiration management.

Clinical protocols

Created the Penicillin Allergy Guideline, Crash Cart Medication Distribution Protocol, and ICU Insulin Infusion Protocol.
